The study finds that substance P-induced increases in inspiratory electromyogram (IntEMG) frequency, amplitude, and minute volume are entirely mediated by NK1 receptors. Blockade of NK1 receptors in the ventral respiratory parafacial region reduces hypercapnia-induced increases in IntEMG activity. Additionally, it confirms that NK1-R of the ventral aspect of the pFRG region co-localize with glutamatergic neurons, suggesting their role in respiratory regulation.
