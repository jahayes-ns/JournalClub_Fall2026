Biophoton emissions have been proposed as a means to detect tumors in animals, monitor diseases like Alzheimer's, and even play a role in various therapeutic processes such as red-light therapies.

However, the research community remains skeptical about the significance of biophoton emissions, with questions surrounding photon counts and their potential physiological effects.
